01

metal-aware-sbdd

The metal is deleted before the model ever sees the pocket.

Published pocket-conditioned 3D generative models — DiffSBDD, TargetDiff, Pocket2Mol — strip catalytic metal ions during preprocessing. Found by reading their source. On a curated 133-target catalytic-zinc benchmark held disjoint from CrossDocked training data, generated ligands coordinate the zinc validly in a fifth of molecules against a 77% native-ligand ceiling. Fine-tuning on metalloprotein-enriched data through the same metal-blind representation makes it worse, not better. Endpoints and decision rules pre-registered before the data they judge; the repair arm is still running.

  • 77.2%native-ligand ceiling
  • 20.0%base DiffSBDD
  • 10.6%after metal-blind fine-tuning
02

strider

Nucleic-acid thermodynamics without NUPACK or ViennaRNA.

Nearest-neighbor free energies, O(n³) secondary-structure folding by Zuker and McCaskill recursions, strand-displacement rate constants, and a defect-weighted parallel-tempered sequence designer — pure Python, no external thermodynamics dependency. Validated against canonical hairpins rather than asserted. Published on PyPI as strider-dna.

  • 0.99mean F-measure, 11 canonical hairpins
  • 10/11exact structure match
  • 646tests passing
03

cadence

Aging as a velocity, not a state.

A latent Neural ODE–VAE over six waves of the Mexican Health and Aging Study, 2001 to 2021, compressing 34 clinical deficits into an 8-dimensional manifold whose native output is aging velocity. Two patients with identical frailty scores can sit in entirely different velocity regimes. Cognitive-decline AUC 0.72 → 0.79 and functional-decline 0.67 → 0.75 over demographics and clinical state under patient-level 5-fold cross-validation; removing ODE integration from the same encoder and risk head costs 0.085 of mortality C-index. A digital-twin module re-integrates the learned dynamics under modified lifestyle controls, behind a conformal Mahalanobis out-of-distribution guardrail.

  • 0.865mortality C-index, full model
  • 0.780same encoder, no ODE
  • 4.77×mortality hazard, fast vs slow agers
04

ATOMICA-Diffusion

Does a pretrained interaction representation beat raw pocket geometry?

Couples frozen ATOMICA pocket embeddings — a foundation model pretrained on roughly two million molecular interaction interfaces — into a frozen DiffSBDD through a single SE(3)-equivariant cross-attention adapter. Both backbones stay frozen, so the measured effect isolates the conditioning signal itself. It buys drug-likeness and costs diversity, which is the honest result rather than the convenient one. Applied to PBP3 in multidrug-resistant A. baumannii, P. aeruginosa and K. pneumoniae.

  • +13.9%QED against unconditioned baseline
  • +6.2%Lipinski compliance
  • −6.4%diversity, the tradeoff
05

mantis-delta

Certify the dynamics from topology, before running anything.

Chemical reaction network theory under mass-action kinetics: deficiency, linkage classes and weak reversibility, then the Deficiency Zero and Deficiency One theorems, with symbolic ODEs and Jacobians derived through SymPy. When a theorem applies you know the qualitative behaviour — unique steady state, no oscillations, no bistability — for every physically admissible rate constant, without running a single simulation. Where no theorem applies, the numerical side takes over and finds the limit cycles.

  • <1%deviation from QSSA, 400× activity scan
  • 10⁻⁶steady-state residual, M s⁻¹
  • 169tests passing
06

Gram-stain pathogen identification

not public

Whole-slide attention over a corpus built with the microbiologists who labelled it.

Self-attention Multiple Instance Learning over Vision Transformer patch embeddings, standardizing 5,828 clinical isolates through 95th-percentile normalization, CLAHE and Otsu thresholding, with multi-head attention modelling spatial context across the whole slide. Built with clinical microbiologists at Hospital Civil de Guadalajara, who set the label-quality and validation design. Manuscript under review; code not yet public.

  • 0.947accuracy, Gram class
  • 0.876accuracy, species level
  • 5,828clinical isolates
