Selected work

metal-aware-sbdd

Published pocket-conditioned 3D generative models — DiffSBDD, TargetDiff, Pocket2Mol — delete catalytic metal ions during preprocessing; found by reading their source. On a curated 133-target catalytic-zinc benchmark held disjoint from CrossDocked training data, generated ligands coordinate the zinc validly in 20% of molecules against a 77% native-ligand ceiling. Fine-tuning on metalloprotein-enriched data through the same metal-blind representation makes it worse, not better: 11%. Endpoints and decision rules pre-registered before the data they judge. Repair arm in progress.

strider

Nucleic acid thermodynamics and kinetics in pure Python, with no NUPACK or ViennaRNA dependency: nearest-neighbor free energies, O(n³) secondary-structure folding (Zuker, McCaskill), strand-displacement rate constants, parallel-tempered sequence design. Mean F-measure 0.99 across 11 canonical hairpins, 10 of 11 an exact match; 0% relative error on toehold rate constants from 0 to 12 nt; 646 tests. On PyPI as strider-dna.

cadence

Latent Neural ODE–VAE over six waves of the Mexican Health and Aging Study (2001–2021), compressing 34 clinical deficits into an 8-dimensional manifold whose native output is aging velocity. Cognitive-decline AUC 0.72 → 0.79 and functional-decline AUC 0.67 → 0.75 over demographics and clinical state, under patient-level 5-fold cross-validation; mortality C-index 0.865 against 0.780 for the same encoder without ODE integration. A digital-twin module re-integrates the learned dynamics under modified lifestyle controls, behind a conformal Mahalanobis out-of-distribution guardrail.

ATOMICA-Diffusion

Does conditioning a structure-based diffusion model on a pretrained interaction representation beat raw pocket geometry? Couples frozen ATOMICA pocket embeddings to frozen DiffSBDD through a single SE(3)-equivariant cross-attention adapter, so the measured effect isolates the conditioning signal. Across 100 held-out pockets and roughly 9,300 valid molecules per arm, QED rises 0.424 → 0.483 and Lipinski 4.42 → 4.69. Target application: PBP3 in multidrug-resistant A. baumannii, P. aeruginosa and K. pneumoniae.

mantis-delta

Chemical reaction network theory under mass-action kinetics: deficiency, linkage classes and weak reversibility, then the Deficiency Zero and Deficiency One theorems, with symbolic ODEs and Jacobians via SymPy. When a theorem applies, qualitative behaviour is certified from topology alone for every admissible rate constant, without running a simulation. Steady-state residuals below 10⁻⁶ M s⁻¹; Goldbeter–Koshland dose-response matches the quasi-steady-state approximation to within 1% across a 400× activity scan; 169 tests.

Gram-stain pathogen identification not public

Self-attention Multiple Instance Learning over Vision Transformer patch embeddings, across 5,828 standardized clinical isolates: 0.947 accuracy on Gram class, 0.876 at species level. Built with clinical microbiologists at Hospital Civil de Guadalajara. Manuscript under review; code not yet public.
